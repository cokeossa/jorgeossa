Why travel elsewhere for theatre when one of the largest stages in Europe is right here, in Olesa de Montserrat? *Focus a la Nit* turns the town's 2023 season into a story told in nine acts: Chanel takes the bus home after a day of climbing in Montserrat, as she has done hundreds of times, but this time the ride becomes a nightmare that slowly reveals one of Catalonia's best-kept secrets.

Each act is a poster, written in Catalan and Spanish, built on a 500-year tradition — La Passió — and on a community that rebuilt its theatre after it burned down in 1983. The campaign closes with an editorial piece printed with ink that glows under UV light.
